    💬 Frequently Asked Blooms

    💙 How do I change my hydrangeas from blue to pink?
    For pink blooms, raise soil pH by adding garden lime (dolomitic) and use a low-aluminum, high-phosphorus fertilizer. Test pH — aim for 6.5–7.0. Transition takes one season.
