🌱 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. They help us recognize your browser, remember your actions and preferences (like language or theme), and analyze how visitors interact with our content. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage and session storage for essential features.

⚙️ 3. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You are in control. Most web browsers allow you to manage cookie preferences through settings. You can block or delete cookies at any time, though blocking essential cookies may affect certain features (like newsletter signups or preference memory). Here’s how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data → customize blocking.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection → Manage exceptions.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Alternatively, you can use our interactive cookie preference tool below (if you change your mind, just reload the page and reconfigure).
