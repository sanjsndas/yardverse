🔒 Privacy & Your Garden Journey

Your data grows safely with YardVerse — transparency, respect, and care for every visitor 🌱

📅 Last revised: April 24, 2026 | Effective: April 30, 2026

Welcome to YardVerse Botanical Space (“YardVerse”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). At YardVerse, we blend the beauty of blue tranquility with pink joy — and that ethos extends to how we handle your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of your information when you visit our website (the “Site”), contact our team, subscribe to our botanical newsletter, or engage with our gardening services.

🌸 Our Bloom Promise: We never sell your personal data. Any information you share helps us cultivate a better gardening experience, offer personalized advice, and notify you about blue hydrangea workshops or pink peony events. Your trust is our most precious seed.

🌼 1. Information We Collect

In order to nurture a personalized botanical space, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address (when you voluntarily provide them via contact forms, event registrations, or newsletter sign-ups).
  • Gardening Preferences: Information you share about your garden style, plant interests (blue vs pink themes), soil conditions, or feedback regarding our content.
  • Communication Data: Messages you send us through the contact form, support requests, or social media interactions (including #YardVerseBloom tags).
  • Automated Technical Data: When you browse our website, we automatically collect standard server logs, IP address, browser type, device information, and referring pages to improve site performance and detect security issues.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use functional and analytics cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors interact with our blue-pink content. For full details, please visit our Cookies Policy.

💧 2. How We Use Your Information

Just as a gardener uses water and sunlight with intent, we use your data for specific, legitimate purposes:

  • 🌸 To respond to your inquiries, questions, or gardening advice requests – typically within 24–48 hours.
  • 💙 To send you seasonal newsletters, blue & pink garden inspiration, workshop announcements, and YardVerse updates (only if you’ve opted in).
  • 📋 To improve our website, analyze usage trends, and enhance the 78,000+ original gardening words available across the ecosystem.
  • 🌿 To process tour reservations, event registrations, or virtual consultation scheduling.
  • 🛡️ To protect the security and integrity of our Site, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

We will never use your personal information for intrusive profiling or automated decision-making that significantly affects you without your explicit consent.

🌺 3. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

For our beloved visitors from the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland, we process your data based on:

  • Consent: When you subscribe to newsletters or provide sensitive gardening preferences.
  • Contractual Necessity: When you request a consultation or register for an event.
  • Legitimate Interests: Improving our website, securing the platform, and responding to inquiries — always balanced with your privacy rights.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law or to protect our rights.

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt-out of any “sale” of data (we do not sell data). To exercise your rights, use the contact details in Section 9 below.

📬 4. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

YardVerse values your trust, so we limit third-party sharing to essential functions:

  • 🌟 Service Providers: We may share data with trusted partners who assist with email delivery (e.g., newsletter platforms), analytics (privacy-focused), or event registration, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • ⚖️ Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or governmental request, we may disclose information to comply with legal processes or protect the safety of YardVerse, our users, or the public.
  • 🌱 Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction. We’ll notify you via prominent notice on our Site.

We never sell or rent your personal data to advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing companies. Period.

🛡️ 5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to databases, and regular security monitoring. However, no online transmission is 100% secure. We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Typically:

  • Inquiry and contact form data: up to 24 months after last interaction, unless you request earlier deletion.
  • Newsletter subscription data: until you unsubscribe (we keep an unsubscribed record to respect your preferences).
  • Website logs: up to 12 months for security analysis.

🍪 6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses essential and analytics cookies to improve navigation, remember your preferences, and understand which garden guides are most popular. We do not use invasive tracking for advertising purposes. You can manage cookie preferences via browser settings; however, disabling cookies may affect functionality. For a detailed list of cookies and choices, please review our separate Cookies Policy.

👧 7. Children’s Privacy

YardVerse Botanical Space is intended for general audiences and gardening enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information promptly.

🌍 8. International Data Transfers

Our headquarters are in the United States (Austin, Texas). If you access our Site from outside the US, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US where our servers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

✉️ 9. Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing: Click “unsubscribe” in any newsletter email or contact us directly.
  • Restriction & Objection: Limit how we use your data under certain circumstances.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

As our garden grows, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any revisions will be posted on this page with a new “Last revised” date. For material changes, we’ll provide a prominent notice on our website or via email (if you’ve subscribed). We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
